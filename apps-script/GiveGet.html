Give / Get

What can you offer students, and what could students do for you? The whole building can see this list. The Trailback Panel uses it to give students a real way to reconnect and re-engage, with you as the adult.

🎁 Give

Something you can offer a student: a lunch group, a mentor check-in, a club seat, tutoring, a quiet spot, a chance to try something.

🙌 Get

Something a student can do for or with you: set up the lab, help in the library, run the morning cart, read to a younger class, help at an event.
